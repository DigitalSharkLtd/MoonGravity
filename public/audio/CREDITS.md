# MOON GRAVITY - audio credits

All recorded samples in this folder are public-domain or CC0. They were trimmed, pitch-shifted, layered,
filtered, loudness-matched and re-encoded (mono MP3) for the game. Everything else you hear
(sub-bass layers, suit sounds, UI stingers, music) is synthesized at runtime with the WebAudio API.
